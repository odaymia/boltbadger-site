What it does

Everything the front counter needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Bolt Badger replaces the tangle of paper tickets, spreadsheets, and dated POS software with one system built around how a service bay actually runs.

Tickets

Work orders & invoicing

Estimate to repair order to invoice on one number. Itemized, plain-language, and California BAR-compliant out of the box.

Customers

Customers & vehicles

Plate-to-VIN decode, full service history per car, and short service codes that show what was done last visit at a glance.

Specs

Automatic fluid specs

Enter the car and the right oil grade, capacity, filter, and gear-oil and coolant specs come up on their own — no manual lookup.

Inventory

Parts & smart reorder

A full parts catalog with sell-through reporting and a reorder planner that tells you what to buy before you run out.

Tires

Sell tires from the bay

Search your distributor's live stock and drop a tire straight onto the ticket at the right price.

Sign-off

Signature pad & kiosk

Customers approve and sign on a locked tablet, then scan a QR to pull up every receipt in their portal.

Portal

Customer portal

Every invoice and service record, on the customer's phone — the loyalty hook that brings them back.

Reports

Reports & commissions

Sales, profit, and per-tech commission payouts — split across the advisor, the top tech, and the pit — done for you.

Reliability

Offline-first, cloud-synced

Keeps working with no internet and syncs every device the moment it's back. The counter never stops.

Our story

We got tired of the tools, so we built our own.

Bolt Badger wasn't dreamed up in a boardroom. It was built inside a working service shop in San Diego, because nothing on the market fit how a busy lube-and-service bay actually runs — and the old systems were slow, clunky, and always trying to lock the data away.

Every feature earned its place on a real counter, on a real busy Saturday, with real customers waiting. That's the difference: this is shop software written by the people standing behind the counter, not for them.

Now we're opening it up to other shops. — The Bolt Badger team, San Diego, CA
